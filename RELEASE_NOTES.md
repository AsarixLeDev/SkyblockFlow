The first public beta of **SkyblockFlow** runs on **Minecraft 26.2**, with **Fabric Loader 0.20.1**, **Fabric API 0.161.0+26.2** and **Java 25**.

Download `skyblockflow-PUBLIC-BETA-0.0.1.jar` and place it in your instance's `mods` folder. Remove the previous mod JAR before starting Minecraft. Open SkyblockFlow with **F7** or `/skyblockflow`. The beta starts with settings in `config/skyblockflow/`.

- **Recipe helper** with a compact inventory item list, recipes and uses, favorites and a raw-material checklist. Open it directly with `/skyblockflowrecipes`.
- **Chat tabs** let you switch between categories without leaving chat.
- **Energy planner** shows whether production covers demand and whether storage runs out overnight.
- **Rocket League tracker** displays available team logos and question marks for teams without a logo.

Discord connection is unavailable while public access is being verified. Shared edits and community names are opt-in.

The sources JAR is for developers. `SHA256SUMS` contains checksums for both JARs.
